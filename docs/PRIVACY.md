# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-14

This Privacy Policy describes how TelePayGate ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you use our services, including the TelePayGate bot and dashboard.

- Data collected: Telegram user ID (when interacting with the bot), payment metadata (amount, timestamp, status), email for dashboard accounts, logs for debugging, and minimal audit data to process payments.
- Purpose: Process payments, monitor deposits, deliver webhooks to developer integrations, provide support, and comply with legal obligations.
- Third parties: We use TON network services, DEX providers (DeDust, Ston.fi) and hosting providers. We do not use centralized exchanges.
- Retention: Payment records and required transaction logs are retained for a minimum of 180 days and up to 7 years where legally required.
- Security: Private keys and mnemonics must be stored encrypted with `WALLET_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Webhook payloads use HMAC signatures via `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET`/`WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
- Your rights: Contact support to request data export, correction, or deletion where applicable.
- Contact: support@example.com

This policy is a project draft — update contact emails, retention periods, and legal text as required before public submission.
